Privacy Policy

JLPT Vocab is a Japanese vocabulary learning app designed to help you prepare for the Japanese-Language Proficiency Test (JLPT). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

👤

Account and personal information

JLPT Vocab does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, or other personal contact information to use its core learning features.

💾

Learning progress and saved data

Currently, your vocabulary study progress, learning preferences, and saved words are stored locally on your device. JLPT Vocab does not currently provide cloud synchronization of this study data. Certain app usage events may be collected through Firebase Analytics, as described below.

Local app data may be removed when you uninstall the app. Device backups, if enabled, are managed by your device and backup provider according to their settings and policies.

📖

Vocabulary and learning features

The app provides vocabulary entries, meanings, and example sentences for study. Your interactions with the learning features are used on your device to support your study experience, such as keeping track of words you have reviewed.

📊

Analytics

JLPT Vocab uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used and improve its features and reliability. Analytics may collect information such as app interactions and usage events, app version, device model, operating system information, approximate geographic information derived from technical signals, and an app instance identifier.

We do not intentionally include the content of your saved vocabulary lists or personal contact information in analytics events. Analytics information is processed by Google as a service provider. For more details, see Firebase Privacy and Security and Google's Privacy Policy.

Analytics data is used to measure feature usage, understand app performance, and prioritize improvements. The information collected may depend on your device settings and the Firebase SDK configuration.

🌐

Internet access and external services

The core vocabulary learning features currently work without uploading your saved vocabulary progress to a JLPT Vocab cloud account. Firebase Analytics may transmit usage and device information to Google. If you open an external website from the app, that website may process information under its own privacy policy.

🚷

Current app practices

  • Require an account for core vocabulary study
  • Sell personal information
  • Access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise location for core vocabulary study
  • Currently offer advertising or cloud synchronization of saved study progress
🔄

Changes to this policy

JLPT Vocab may introduce additional features in the future, such as cloud synchronization, advertising, or in-app purchases. These features are not described as active services in this policy. Before introducing any feature that materially changes how personal data is collected, used, or shared, we will update this policy and any applicable in-app or App Store privacy disclosures.
